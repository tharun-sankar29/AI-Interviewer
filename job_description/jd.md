# Junior Mechanical Engineer

## About the Role

We are looking for a motivated **Junior Mechanical Engineer** to join our engineering team. This is an entry-level opportunity for a B.Tech/B.E. Mechanical Engineering graduate or final-year student who is interested in design, manufacturing, and engineering problem-solving.

## Responsibilities

* Assist with mechanical design and engineering projects.
* Create and review basic engineering drawings and documentation.
* Support product development and manufacturing activities.
* Perform basic calculations, analysis, and troubleshooting.
* Collaborate with senior engineers and cross-functional teams.
* Participate in testing, prototyping, and process improvement activities.

## Requirements

* B.Tech/B.E. in Mechanical Engineering.
* Basic understanding of mechanical engineering principles.
* Familiarity with engineering drawings and CAD concepts.
* Good analytical and problem-solving skills.
* Willingness to learn new tools and technologies.
* Good communication and teamwork skills.

## Preferred Skills

* AutoCAD, SolidWorks, or similar CAD software.
* Basic knowledge of manufacturing processes.
* Knowledge of materials and mechanical design.
* Basic understanding of quality and testing processes.

## Experience

**0–2 years** of experience. Fresh graduates and final-year students are welcome to apply.

## Location

Chennai, Tamil Nadu

## Employment Type

Full-time, Entry Level
